## Report a bug

Choose **Help ▸ Report a Problem…** in Shift. It opens a bug report form on [GitHub](https://github.com/shift-editor/shift/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). You need a GitHub account to submit it.

A good report includes:

- your Shift version (in **About Shift**) and your operating system
- the steps that cause the problem, and what you expected to happen instead
- screenshots or a short screen recording
- the font file, or a small font that shows the problem, if you can share it
- Shift’s logs: choose **Help ▸ Show Logs** and attach the latest log file

## Ask for help or share feedback

- Join the [Shift Discord](https://discord.gg/582FxBdNH7) to ask questions and talk to other users.
- Choose **Help ▸ Feedback…** to tell us what you’d like Shift to do.
